# Meteor Cafe — development status

**Selected Android baseline:** 1.1.0 (`versionCode 2`)

## Confirmed on the reference device

- The dedicated application was built and installed on a Samsung Galaxy A05.
- Meteor Cafe launched and displayed its menus and gameplay.
- The game was centered on the portrait display.
- The original app title and logo were retained.
- The outer display area was filled with a softened background extension.
- The four coloured order buttons and corresponding touch areas received an 8-pixel logical vertical adjustment.
