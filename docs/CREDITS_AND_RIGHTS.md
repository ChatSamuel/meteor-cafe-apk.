# Credits and rights history

## Original game credits

The in-game credits found in the examined Meteor Cafe JAR identify:

| Role | Credit shown in the game |
|---|---|
| Copyright notice | © 2010 |
| Developer and producer | **3 Dynamics (Asia) Limited** |
| Distributor | **THQ** |

The JAR manifest also contains `MIDlet-Vendor: THQ` and `Created-By: Metaflow`. The `Created-By` field is a build-tool or production metadata field; it does not identify an individual game designer or establish legal authorship. The examined game materials do not provide a verifiable list of individual developers.

The original team and companies are credited here according to the material found in the game. No individual contributor is attributed without supporting evidence.

## Corporate acquisition history

Public corporate filings document the following history:

1. **December 2013:** the corporate group then known as Fairson Holdings Limited completed the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of 3 Dynamics (Asia) Limited through its wholly owned subsidiary, Dynamic Miracle Limited. The group's 2015 annual report records the completion of this acquisition in December 2013.
2. **October 2021:** the listed parent changed its English name from China Oil Gangran Energy Group Holdings Limited to **Century Energy International Holdings Limited**.
3. **2022 annual report:** Century Energy's subsidiary schedule listed 3 Dynamics (Asia) Limited as an indirectly wholly owned subsidiary and described its principal activity as inactive.

These records establish the corporate acquisition and subsequent company-name history. A share acquisition of a company does not, on its own, prove that copyright in a particular game was assigned, licensed or remains with a specific entity. The current ownership and licensing status of Meteor Cafe have not been independently established by the records cited here.
